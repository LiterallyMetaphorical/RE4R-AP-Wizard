BioRand for Resident Evil 4 Remake
https://github.com/IntelOrca/biorand-re4r

Every release of this project ships a compiled build of a BioRand fork as
assets/BioRand/biorand-re4r.exe. BioRand is MIT licensed, and MIT requires its
copyright and permission notice to travel with any copy or substantial portion
of the software, so that notice is reproduced in full below.

Which build is bundled, and what the fork changes, is recorded alongside this
file in BIORAND_PROVENANCE.txt.
